Welcome to LPU ID Card Reissue Portal

This portal allows Lovely Professional University students to apply for a replacement or reissue of their Student Identity Card. Please keep your Roll Number and required documents ready before applying.

📄 What You Can Do

  • Apply for ID card reissue (Lost / Damaged / Defaced)
  • Upload required document checklist
  • Choose Standard or Urgent processing
  • Track your application status in real time

📋 Required Documents

  • Lost: Police Lost Report Acknowledgment
  • Damaged: Old Damaged Card Submission
  • Defaced: Old Damaged Card Submission

💵 Processing Fees (Demo)

  • Standard Reissue — ₹200
  • Urgent 24-Hour Processing — ₹500
