General Instructions & Guidelines

Welcome to the LPU Student ID Card Reissue Portal. This portal is for students of Lovely Professional University who need a replacement ID Card due to loss, damage, or defacement.

1. Reasons for Reissue

  • Lost Card: ID card is missing or stolen.
  • Damaged Card: Card is physically broken or barcode is unreadable.
  • Defaced Card: Photo or text has faded over time.

2. Required Documents

  • Lost: Police Complaint / DDR copy acknowledgment.
  • Damaged/Defaced: Original old ID card must be submitted.
  • All: Valid Student Registration / Roll Number.

3. Collection Process

  • Standard processing takes 7 to 10 working days.
  • Urgent processing is completed within 24 to 48 hours.
  • Collect the card from Block 30 (Division of Student Welfare).
